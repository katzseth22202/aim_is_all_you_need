# One standoff volume, and it is the column's

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-09-05

## Context

Balloon-Pulse-Propulsion commit `85f29ec` carried reply **R14**, *"Column length
and bag volume resolve in your favour. We adopt 23.8 m and 672.9 m³"*, and left
this repository an explicit debt:

> `tab:axial_bag` is generated by `make bag-state` in `aim_is_all_you_need`, and
> we recomputed it **by hand** from the model its own caption states... The
> generator has **not** been rerun, so the paper's table and its generator now
> disagree. That sits alongside the `tab:bag_state` recompute already owed for
> the ice-enthalpy defects. **Both want one pass.**

The ice-enthalpy half landed here as ADR 0027 and was written up for the paper
in `docs/paper_corrections_bag_state_2026-09-04.md`; the paper has not applied
it yet. This ADR is the other half, and the pass is the one R14 asked for.

### The disagreement R14 resolved

The paper *stated* a 23 m column of 660 m³ and *quoted*, in prose around it, a
3.0 m bore, a 28 m² cross-section and an aspect ratio of 4. Those three round
figures are not roundings of the stated pair. They are roundings of
`puffsat_impact_simulation`'s pair:

| | paper as stated | paper as quoted | sim, 23.8 m / 672.9 m³ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| bore, `sqrt(V/(pi l))` | 3.022 m | "3.0 m" | **3.000 m** |
| cross-section | 28.70 m² | "28 m²" | **28.27 m²** |
| aspect `l/2r` | 3.81 | "4" | **3.97** |

The paper adopted the sim's pair. This repository had **both numbers in it at
once**, and in one case inside a single module:

- `bag_state.BAG_VOLUME` was 659.6 m³ — the 5.4 m sphere of `tab:bag_sizing` —
  with `AXIAL_BAG_LENGTHS` carrying a 23.0 m flown row and `SPHERE_BORE_RADIUS`
  a hardcoded 5.40 m.
- `nozzle_geometry` carried `BORE_RADIUS = 3.0` and `COLUMN_LENGTH = 23.8`, the
  sim's pair, **and** `BAG_VOLUME = 659.6`. So `full_bore_slug_ratio()` swept
  672.9 m³ of column at a density computed from 659.6 and returned **8.69** for
  a quantity whose definition is `m_slug / m_impactor` = 213/25 = **8.52**.

## Decision

**There is one bag, its volume is the column's own `pi r^2 l`, and everything
that needs a volume takes it from there.**

1. `bag_state.BAG_VOLUME` is **derived** as `pi * 3.00^2 * 23.8` = 672.93 m³,
   which the paper prints as 672.9. `SPHERE_BORE_RADIUS` is now *derived*
   from it — `(3V/4pi)^(1/3)` = 5.4361 m — rather than written down, because the
   table normalises its conductor column to "the sphere of the same volume" and
   a hardcoded 5.40 stopped being that sphere the moment the volume moved.
   `SPHERE_BAG_LENGTH` and `FLOWN_COLUMN_LENGTH` (23.8 m) join it, and
   `AXIAL_BAG_LENGTHS` is assembled from them.
2. `nozzle_geometry.BAG_VOLUME` is derived the same way from its own
   `BORE_RADIUS` and `COLUMN_LENGTH`, which is the whole of the fix there. The
   two modules now agree to the last bit rather than to the printed digit.
3. **The thermodynamic state moves with the geometry.** `tab:bag_state` is the
   mist that fills this bag, so it is solved at 672.9 m³ rather than at the 5.4 m
   sphere's 659.6. Adopting the geometry and not the state would rebuild exactly
   the split R14 was closing.
4. **Vendored inputs from `puffsat_impact_simulation` do not move**, because
   this repository cannot re-solve them. `plume_state.FLOWN_BAG_DENSITY` stays
   0.323 (= 213/659.6), as do the conductivity fits, the cliff temperatures,
   `data/plume_state.csv`, and `nozzle_geometry`'s shocked-sound-speed table.
   `nozzle_geometry.SIM_SOLVE_DENSITY` names that density, and the sweeps take
   an optional `slug_density` so the cross-repo pins stay exact instead of
   being loosened or deleted.

## Consequences

### `tab:axial_bag` now reproduces from the generator — except one column

`make bag-state`. Bore, conductor, `F` and area match the paper at `85f29ec`
cell for cell:

| length | bore | conductor | `F` | area | pressure film |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10.9 m (sphere) | 5.44 m | 1.00 | 1.50 | 371 m² | **4.84 kg** (paper: 4.9) |
| 16 m | 3.66 m | 0.99 | 1.82 | 368 m² | **5.88 kg** (paper: 6.0) |
| 23.8 m | 3.00 m | 1.21 | 1.91 | 449 m² | **6.16 kg** (paper: 6.3) |
| 32 m | 2.59 m | 1.40 | 1.94 | 520 m² | **6.27 kg** (paper: 6.4) |
| 50 m | 2.07 m | 1.75 | 1.97 | 650 m² | **6.37 kg** (paper: 6.5) |

**The paper's hand-recomputed Pressure column is high by the volume ratio.**
Multiplying the model by 672.93/659.6 = 1.0202 reproduces all five printed
cells to the digit — 4.94, 6.00, 6.29, 6.40, 6.50 against 4.9, 6.0, 6.3, 6.4,
6.5 — which is what identifies the error. `eq:bag_film_mass` is
`F x rho_f R_g T / (M sigma)` and **volume does not appear**: the film sizes a
vessel holding `PV = n R_g T`, and a bigger bag drops `P` by exactly what it
adds to `V`. Scaling the film by the new volume charges the change twice — once
in `V`, and once already inside the `P` the old volume produced. The honest
effect runs the *other* way and is a tenth of the size: the same vapour spread
2% more thinly saturates 0.4 K colder, and the film is linear in `T`.

Pinned as `test_the_papers_hand_recomputed_pressure_column_is_high_by_the_volume`
rather than left as prose, because the paper still prints the column.

Two of those are visible at the paper's printed precision and are new
corrections owed: the Earth column's **pressure is 8.5 kPa, not 8.7**, and its
**film is 4.8 kg, not 4.9**. The mist is 316 K either way.

The 8.5 is worth a note. The paper already prints both numbers for this one row
— `sec:needle_through_fog` says the bag "holds at most 8.7 kPa" while
`sec:watering_it_down` compares argon's 881 Pa against "the 8.5 kPa of the steam
bag". After this pass 8.5 is the right one, so a second internal disagreement
closes on its own. Nothing here establishes that the 8.5 was *derived* this way;
it has one commit of provenance and may simply have been a slip.

### The handling floor rises with the area, and so does what it protects

The flown column's membrane goes from 437 m² to **449 m²**, so the floor at
6–25 µm is **2.5–10.3 kg** rather than 2.4–10.0, and 12.7 µm of polyethylene is
**5.24 kg** rather than 5.1. Both conclusions the floor carries are unmoved and
both get slightly more room: the cold column's pressure film is 0.51–0.68 kg
across the five lengths, and the Earth column with the plug is 4.45 kg against a
5.24 kg floor, so it is still handling-governed.

### `nozzle_geometry`: the full-bore sweep becomes exact

- `full_bore_slug_ratio()` = **8.520**, which is `BAG_SLUG_MASS / IMPACTOR_MASS`
  identically, because the density and the swept volume are now the same bag.
  It was 8.69. R14 states the same thing from the paper's side: "`k` = 8.52,
  not 8.5 (213/25), with 8.5 kept as the round figure."
- Bag density **0.3165 kg/m³** (was 0.3229). Both print as the paper's 0.32.
- The self-widening front delivers **7.10** from the design 0.15 m arrival and
  **8.43** from 0.8 of the bore, against 7.21 and 8.60. ADR 0016's tolled
  optimum band is 6.75–7.77, so the compact arrival is still inside it with more
  room than before, and the wide front still overshoots. **The verdict does not
  move; it gets stronger.**
- `arrival_fraction_for(6.75)` = **0.890**, was 0.881.
- Mirror stagnation at the throat end: ratio **1.174** (unchanged, it is a
  ratio), wall **22.7 MPa** (still "23 MPa"), field **7.55 T** — the paper's
  7.6 T becomes **7.5 T**.
- `confinement_field(4.427e9)` now defaults to the column and gives **4.07 T**.
  `tab:bag_sizing`'s 4.11 T is its 5.4 m *sphere* and is asked for by name.

### What the companion sim is now owed

`puffsat_impact_simulation` solved the plume state, the conductivity fits, the
cliff temperatures and the shocked sound speeds at **0.323 kg/m³**, which is
213 kg in the *old* volume. The adopted bag is 2% thinner. Nothing here suggests
that matters — the sound-speed table moves 9% across an eightfold span in
compression, so 2% is a fortieth of a known-small sensitivity — but it is a
mismatch between a vendored input and the geometry it is used with, and it is
recorded rather than assumed away. The pins against those runs are evaluated at
`SIM_SOLVE_DENSITY` so they stay exact and stay honest about which bag they are.

## Reproducing

```bash
make bag-state      # tab:bag_state, tab:bag_sizing, tab:axial_bag, the plug
make nozzle-geom    # the sweep, the mirror trade, the two-term nozzle mass
make bag-converge   # cut 4 and the optical-depth argument
pytest tests/test_bag_state.py tests/test_nozzle_geometry.py \
       tests/test_bag_converge.py tests/test_plume_state.py -s
```

Inputs, so this does not depend on scratch state. The adopted geometry is a
23.8 m column at a 3.00 m bore, from `puffsat_impact_simulation` by way of paper
reply R14; its volume is `pi * 3.0^2 * 23.8` = 672.9 m³ and its equal-volume
sphere is 5.4361 m. The slug is 213 kg, the impactor 25 kg, the plug 37.5 kg.
Everything else — the 2.54% solved leak at the 45.58 km/s cold end, the
0.570 MJ/kg melting gate, the 2.36 MJ/kg latent heat, Magnus–Tetens — is ADR
0027's and unchanged.
